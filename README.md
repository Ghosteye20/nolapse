NoLapse keeps every bill, renewal, subscription and expiry date in one private place, right in Chrome's side panel, and reminds you before anything lapses.

WHY NOLAPSE
Bills and renewals are scattered: card statements, insurance emails, subscription receipts, a passport in a drawer. NoLapse brings them together, shows what needs attention first, and alerts you in good time.

EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
• Overdue, due this week and due in the next 30 days, sorted by date
• A running total of what is coming up
• Filter by category with one click: cards & loans, insurance, vehicle, home & bills, subscriptions, tax & government, investments, education
• Search across all your reminders

ADD A REMINDER IN SECONDS
• 50+ ready-made templates with sensible alert days already set
• India pack: PUC certificate, FASTag, vehicle RC, PPF and NPS deadlines, tax-saving (80C), ITR, advance tax instalments, rent agreement renewal
• Or create your own: any name, any date, any repeat

ALERTS ON YOUR SCHEDULE
• Several alerts per reminder, from months ahead to the day itself (useful for passports, licences and insurance)
• Desktop notifications even when the side panel is closed
• Catches up if your computer was off on the day of an alert
• Overdue items keep reminding you daily until they are done
• Snooze for a day, three days or a week
• Badge on the toolbar icon shows how many items are due within 7 days

BUILT FOR REAL BILLS
• Fixed amounts, amounts that vary (like card bills), or no amount at all
• Repeats monthly, quarterly, yearly, every 28 days, every 11 months, every 10 years and more
• Month-end dates handled correctly (31st, then 28 Feb, then 31 Mar)
• Grace periods and auto-pay "keep the balance ready" reminders
• Mark paid in one click: the reminder moves to its next date and the payment is recorded
• Payment history for every reminder, with Undo if you click by mistake

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in
• Your reminders stay in Chrome on your computer. NoLapse makes no network requests of its own
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Never asks for card, policy or ID numbers. A nickname like "Card ··1234" is enough
• Hide amounts with one click when screen-sharing

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
• Export a backup file and import it again, or on another computer
• Export reminders and payment history to Excel (CSV)
• Delete everything at any time

ALSO
• India and Global modes: rupees with lakh/crore formatting, or your own currency
• Light, dark and automatic themes
• Optional "Add to Google Calendar" button (off by default)

HOW TO USE
1. Click the NoLapse icon in the toolbar to open it in the side panel
2. Choose India or Global
3. Click + Add, pick a template (or create your own) and set the due date
4. NoLapse alerts you before it's due. Click "Mark paid" when it's done

PERMISSIONS
• Storage: saves your reminders and settings on your computer
• Alarms: runs the daily check at the time you choose
• Notifications: shows desktop alerts when something is due
• Side panel: shows NoLapse beside the page you're on
NoLapse cannot read or change the websites you visit.
